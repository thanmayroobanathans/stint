    Race strategy (settings)
    Why 90 minutes?

    Sleep researcher Nathaniel Kleitman proposed a roughly 90-minute "basic rest–activity cycle" that keeps running through the day, not just during sleep. The idea: you get one strong wave of alertness, then a natural dip where your brain wants a break. Working with that wave — one deep 90-minute stint, then 15–20 minutes genuinely off — tends to beat grinding through the dip.

    Honest caveat: the evidence for neat daytime 90-minute cycles is mixed, and people vary. Treat 90/20 as a starting race strategy. If your focus fades at 60–70 minutes, shorten the stint in settings.

    Make breaks real recovery: stand up, drink water, look at something far away, and skip the phone — scrolling doesn't let your attention reset.
